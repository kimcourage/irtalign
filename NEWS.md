# irtalign 0.0.0.9000

* `align_irt()`, `align_fits()` and `align_pars()` align group-specific estimates of 2PL, 3PL, graded response and generalized partial credit items, mixed in one analysis.
* FIXED and FREE alignment with an automatically chosen or user-given reference group, and results in the reference-group or product-of-variances metric.
* Standard errors of means, variances and aligned parameters by implicit differentiation and the delta method.
* Noninvariance detection by parameter and group, invariance R-squared, simplicity function contributions and factor mean comparisons through `summary()`, `invariance_table()` and `mean_comparison()`.
